About New Hope

Charlotte’s first Asian church.

Founded in 1977, Charlotte New Hope Church is celebrating 49 years of worship, prayer, fellowship, and ministry.

Our story

49 years rooted in prayer and reaching with hope.

Founded in 1977 as Charlotte’s first Asian church, Charlotte New Hope Church is a Christian community at 701 Scaleybark Road. For 49 years, the church has welcomed people from every background to worship Jesus, grow through Scripture, build strong families, and serve others.

Under the leadership of Pastor John, New Hope continues to serve Charlotte while building ministry relationships across the United States, Asia, and Europe.
